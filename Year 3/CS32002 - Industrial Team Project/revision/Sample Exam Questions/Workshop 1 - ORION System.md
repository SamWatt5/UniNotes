### Executive Brief - ORION: Emergency Response Coordination System

The Executive Leadership Team has authorised the rapid development of ORION, a digital platform intended to support coordination between emergency services during major incidents.

ORION is designed to allow police, fire, and medical responders to share live status updates, allocate resources, and track incident developments in real time. The system is expected to improve situational awareness and reduce delays caused by fragmented communication channels.

The project has been initiated following several high-profile incidents where post-event reviews highlighted poor information flow between agencies. In response, senior leadership has committed to delivering a pilot version of ORION within six weeks, in time for a national emergency preparedness exercise.

Initial requirements were gathered through a series of workshops and documented in a detailed specification. However, different agencies have since raised concerns that their operational needs are not fully reflected. Some stakeholders prioritise speed and flexibility, while others emphasise auditability, control, and strict procedural compliance.

Midway through early development planning, a regulatory review identified additional governance requirements related to data retention and accountability. Senior management have requested that these requirements be incorporated without delaying the delivery timeline. At the same time, they have asked the team to produce a comprehensive delivery plan with fixed milestones and formal sign-off points before implementation begins.

There is agreement that ORION is strategically important, but no shared understanding of which features are essential for the pilot and which can be deferred.
