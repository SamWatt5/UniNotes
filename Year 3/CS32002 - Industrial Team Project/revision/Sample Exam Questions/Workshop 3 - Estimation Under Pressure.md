### Scenario
A regional authority has commissioned a new **Emergency Incident Coordination System (EICS)** to support police, ambulance, and fire services during major incidents.

The system will allow:

- field responders to submit live incident updates,
- control room operators to monitor resource deployment,
- senior commanders to view situational summaries,
- and government oversight officials to review post-incident reports.

Three epics have been agreed:

1. Incident Reporting and Live Updates
2. Operational Coordination and Resource Management
3. Regulatory Oversight and Post-Incident Reporting

Your team was asked to provide an initial delivery estimate in order to align funding and procurement timelines. The estimate was produced after reviewing a set of clearly written user stories and conducting high-level relative sizing. The team expressed moderate confidence.

However, during a follow-up review, senior stakeholders raised concerns. The system must operate during periods of degraded connectivity, support secure multi-agency access, and produce traceable audit records for legal and regulatory review.

Selected user stories were then broken down into detailed task lists. This revealed additional work related to authentication, data validation, incident logging, error handling, synchronisation across agencies, and compliance reporting.

As a result, several story sizes were revised upward, and the overall delivery estimate increased significantly. Confidence in the revised estimate is lower but considered more realistic.

The authority has asked your team to explain what changed and how estimation should be approached in future.

### Questions
#### **1) Causes of estimation change (6 marks)**
Identify two reasons why the team’s initial estimate was significantly lower than the revised estimate.

For each reason:

- Refer explicitly to elements in the scenario.
- Explain why this issue commonly arises during early-stage estimation.
#### **2) Role of task decomposition (5 marks)**
Using the scenario, explain how breaking selected user stories into task-level work improved the team’s understanding of delivery effort.

Your answer should:

- Refer to specific risks or complexities implied in the scenario.
- Explain why story-level estimation alone was insufficient.
#### **3) Improving future estimation practice (4 marks)**
Propose **two structured changes** the team should implement in future estimation exercises for safety-critical systems.

For each change:

- Explain how it addresses weaknesses observed in the scenario.
- Justify why it would improve the credibility of delivery estimates.
