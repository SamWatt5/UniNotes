### Scenario
Following the establishment of the Mars Discovery Living Lab, Star Command has launched the first operational phase of the facility. Visitors can now travel directly to Mars via the automatic transport tunnel and enter the environmental containment bubble without risk.

Early pilot sessions have revealed mixed outcomes.

General visitors report feeling overwhelmed by the volume of scientific information available. School groups struggle to understand how their activities contribute to real research. Meanwhile, several professional scientists have expressed concern that public participation may compromise research integrity or misinterpret preliminary data.

In response, Star Command has requested a focused initial release that:

- Enables visitors to meaningfully contribute to selected citizen science activities,
- Preserves research quality and data integrity,
- Makes uncertainty visible without causing panic or confusion.

However, the scope of the initial release is unclear. Some team members have suggested building a comprehensive participation dashboard, live research feeds, and a contribution ranking system. Others argue that the team should begin with a single, well-defined research activity and expand incrementally. 
You are part of the Agile team responsible for shaping this initial release.
### Questions
#### **1) Identifying user value (4 marks)**
Identify two distinct user roles in this scenario and, for each role, clearly explain one primary goal they are likely to have within the Living Lab.

Your answer should demonstrate an understanding of user-centred thinking rather than technical features.
#### **2) Writing and evaluating user stories (6 marks)**
Write **two user stories** that would be appropriate for the initial release described in the scenario.

For each story:

- Use correct user story structure.
- Explain briefly how it meets at least three elements of INVEST.
- Identify one potential weakness or risk in the story.

#### **3) Managing uncertainty (5 marks)**
One of the proposed features is a live research feed showing evolving data in real time.

(i) Explain why this feature may be difficult to estimate or commit to early in development. (2 marks)

(ii) Describe one appropriate spike that could be used to reduce uncertainty before committing to this feature. Your answer should clearly state:

- the question being investigated,
- the time-box,
- and the expected outcome. (3 marks)
