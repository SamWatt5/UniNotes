2. One source of complexity present in the ORION project is the fact that there is many different stakeholders needing different requirements met for the project. This creates difficulty for the delivery team since they need to implement a wide array of features to make sure that all stakeholders needs are met. An agile approach would be better suited to handling this issue since it allows the delivery team to be in constant communication with all stakeholders, making sure their needs are met throughout the delivery window, instead of some needs not being met at the start, and staying unmet throughout. Another source of complexity for the delivery team is the fact that senior management are requesting new requirements to be incorporated without delaying the delivery timeline. This creates difficulty for the team since it the addition of new requirements will add more development time to the project. Agile is better than a traditional approach for this issue since it allows responding to change, such as the addition of new requirements to happen seamlessly, instead of having to develop the whole system, and incorporate these new requirements at the end.
