### Scenario
A mid-sized fintech company is developing a new fraud detection dashboard for internal analysts. The product is being built using Scrum with two-week sprints. The team is currently halfway through Sprint 3 of a six-sprint delivery timeline.

Sprint 3 commitment includes:

- Real-time transaction alert feed
- Risk score visualisation panel
- Analyst case-note logging
- Performance optimisation for high-volume queries

During the sprint, three significant events occur:

1. A competitor announces a new AI-powered fraud prediction feature that has received strong industry attention.
2. The company’s Chief Risk Officer emails the team stating that regulators have requested additional audit trail transparency following a recent industry investigation.
3. One developer reports that the real-time alert feed is more technically complex than originally estimated and may require architectural rework.

Senior management informally suggests that “we may need to rethink what we’re delivering this sprint.”

The sprint review is scheduled in five days.
### Question
You are asked to advise the team.

Write a structured response addressing the following:

(a) As Product Owner, explain how you would respond to these events during the current sprint.

Your answer should clearly distinguish between what you would change immediately, what you would defer, and how you would manage stakeholder pressure. (12 marks)

(b) As Scrum Master, explain how you would respond to these events during the current sprint.

Your answer should focus on protecting team stability, handling external pressure, and maintaining appropriate Scrum boundaries. (8 marks)

(c) Briefly explain why confusing the responsibilities of Product Owner and Scrum Master in this situation would increase project risk. (5 marks)
