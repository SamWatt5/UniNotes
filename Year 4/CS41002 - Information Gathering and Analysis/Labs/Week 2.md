# Activity 1

| **No.** | **Scenario**                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1       | The University IT team wants to find out **why** students fail to set up multi-factor authentication on their accounts, and at **which step** they get stuck.                         |
| 2       | A researcher wants to understand how computing students **feel** about using generative AI for coursework, including worries they might not want to share in front of other students. |
| 3       | A fitness app company wants to learn how people fit exercise tracking into their **daily routines over a month**.                                                                     |
| 4       | A council is designing an online bin-collection booking service and wants residents to **discuss and react to three early design ideas together**.                                    |
| 5       | A researcher wants to understand how nurses **actually use** a ward’s electronic patient record during a busy shift, including workarounds they might not think to mention.           |
| 6       | The School wants a **quick picture** of how satisfied all 400 Level 1 students are with the new virtual learning environment, and which features they use most.                       |

| **No.** | **Method**                                 | **One reason**                                            | **One limitation**                                   |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| 1       | **think-aloud / usability study**          | See where individuals get stuck in real-time              | time-consuming                                       |
| 2       | **interview**                              | One-on-one - can really hear their thoughts               | time-consuming; interviewer bias                     |
| 3       | **diary study**                            | Over time thoughts; don't forget thoughts by end of study | Unreliable; lots of data; people can drop out easily |
| 4       | **focus group**                            | Community discussion                                      | Loud people squash opinion                           |
| 5       | **observation** (with limited interaction) |                                                           |                                                      |
| 6       | **questionnaire**                          |                                                           |                                                      |
